As businesses prepare for year-end, finance teams face a busy period of closing out accounts, paying vendors, and preparing for the next fiscal year. Planning ahead can help finance teams avoid the payment mistakes that often surface during busy periods. Even minor errors can create delays, reconciliation issues, and extra work later.
