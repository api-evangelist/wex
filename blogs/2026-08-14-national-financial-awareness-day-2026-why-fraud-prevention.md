National Financial Awareness Day is observed every year on August 14. It’s a prompt to revisit budgets, savings goals, and long-term financial plans. But in 2026, there’s one topic that belongs right alongside the usual financial wellness conversation — and it doesn’t get nearly enough airtime: fraud prevention.
