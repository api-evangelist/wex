With the rise of cyber threats and data breaches, and with check fraud still an issue today, safeguarding your financial information is critical. Virtual cards, which use tokenized virtual card numbers (VCNs) have gained popularity, and for the right reasons. VCNs provide users with a dynamic and secure method of making digital payments.
